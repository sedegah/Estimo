## Short description (max 208 characters)

Maintain the ESTIMO material catalogue and prices. Publish a price once and every estimate that uses that material follows, with photos and specifications in one place.

## Description

ESTIMO Admin is the console behind the ESTIMO estimating app. It is the place where the
material catalogue is maintained: prices, photos, specifications and units for every
item an electrician can put into an estimate.

**Prices that stay in step.** Edit a price in the table, publish, and every estimate
that uses that material picks it up. Draft changes can be reviewed, discarded or
published in one action, so a typo never reaches a live quotation.

**A catalogue with pictures.** Each material carries its own photo, specification and
search keywords, and the console shows them exactly as the website does. Add a material
with its photo, unit and starting price in a single page, or delete one together with
its price history.

**Built for how estimates are priced.** Base units (per metre, per length, per piece),
quality tiers and quick-build relevance are part of every material, so the estimating
app can choose sensibly between comparable items.

**Always current.** The console is served from ESTIMO's own infrastructure, so interface
improvements arrive as soon as they ship — reload with Ctrl+R. The desktop shell updates
itself from our release feed.

Sign in with your ESTIMO administrator account. The console is intended for authorised
staff of ESTIMO and its partners.
