# ESTIMO — launch video plan

## The nine answers

1. **What it is.** ESTIMO turns a description of a building in Ghana into a first electrical estimate:
   lights, sockets, board, cabling and labour, with material prices the team maintains.
2. **The claim.** “Picture it lit. Then price it.” Planning an electrical job stops being guesswork.
3. **Visual hook.** The glowing floor plan on navy: amber light points, dashed circuit runs, the lit rooms.
4. **What to show.** Three real moments: counting rooms, searching materials (which honestly show
   “Pending” where no price exists), and the estimate card with its breakdown.
5. **Shortest satisfying cut.** 15 seconds: six scenes, 2.4 seconds each with 0.6 second crossfades.
6. **Tone.** `polished`, with a quiet-premium direction: navy, plaster, one amber accent, large type.
7. **Audio.** Warm corporate bed from the bundled track, gentle fade in and out, no narration.
8. **Share copy.** “Picture it lit. Then price it. Electrical estimates for Ghana, with the prices your
   team actually uses.”
9. **User flow.** Pick a 2-bedroom home → count rooms → search a material → read the estimate.

## Format

1080 × 1920 vertical, 30 fps, 15 seconds, H.264 with AAC. Built for a phone: type is large, safe margins
of 96 px, and every line stays on screen long enough to read.

Transitions are 0.6 second crossfades; each still has a slow push-in so the frame never sits dead.

## Notes on honesty

- The estimate total (GH₵16,800) is the calibrated two-bedroom anchor the app actually produces.
- The material list in scene 3 shows “Pending”, because that is what the live app shows where no price
  exists. The two prices in the console scene are illustrative demo values, not published data.
- No real customer data, no internal URLs, and the site address is never shown or spoken.
