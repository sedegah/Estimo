# ESTIMO Admin

This is the program the ESTIMO team uses to keep material prices and the material list correct.
It is for staff only. It is not for customers.

## Installing

1. Open **ESTIMO-Admin-Setup** from the copy you were given.
2. Accept the terms. If you want it somewhere else, choose the folder.
3. Leave **Open ESTIMO Admin now** ticked and press Finish.

You will find it on your desktop and in the Start menu. You do not need to be an administrator on
the computer to install it.

## Signing in

Sign in with the ESTIMO staff account you were given. If you do not have one, ask first. Only staff
accounts can change prices or add materials.

## Changing prices

Search for a material, type the new price, look over your changes, then save them. Anything you
leave empty stays as it was.

## Adding materials

If something is missing, add it with its name, the group it belongs to and the unit it is sold in
(each, roll, metre). You can also correct the unit of a material that is already there.

## Planning a job

Choose the kind of building, then place lights, sockets, boards and cable runs on the plan. The
first estimate adds up as you go. You can move things around, and remove them if you change your
mind.

## Updates

The program looks for a newer version when it opens, and offers it to you. You can also look
yourself from the **Admin** menu, then **Check for updates**.

Choosing to update keeps your shortcuts and your work exactly as they are. If you are busy, choose
Later and it will ask again next time.

## Removing it

Open Windows **Settings**, then **Apps**, find **ESTIMO Admin** and choose Uninstall. Anything you
saved stays where it is.

## A note on prices

Prices here are planning figures, not a supplier’s live prices. They change. An estimate built
from them is a guide, not a quotation.

## Getting help

If a price will not save, the program will not open, or an update does not arrive, tell the ESTIMO
team which screen you were on and what happened.
